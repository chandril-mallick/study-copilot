# Dabba AI - User Guide

## Welcome to Dabba AI

Dabba AI is your intelligent study companion that helps you learn smarter, not harder. Whether you're a student looking for homework help or a teacher creating engaging lessons, Dabba AI provides AI-powered tools to enhance your educational experience.

## Getting Started

### Accessing Dabba AI

1. **Open your web browser** and navigate to the application URL
2. **Ensure Ollama is running** in the background (the app will show connection status)
3. **Upload your study materials** or start using the AI tools directly

### System Requirements

- Modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)
- Internet connection for initial setup
- Local AI processing (no data leaves your device)

## Core Features

### 1.  AI Assistant Chat

**What it does:** Have natural conversations with an AI assistant for study help, concept explanations, and problem-solving.

**How to use:**

1. Click on the **"Chat"** tab in the main interface
2. Type your question or topic in the chat input
3. Press **Enter** or click **Send**
4. The AI will respond with helpful explanations and guidance

**Example conversations:**

```
You: "Explain quantum physics in simple terms"
AI: "Quantum physics is the branch of physics that deals with the behavior of matter and energy at the smallest scales..."

You: "Help me solve this calculus problem: ∫2x dx"
AI: "The integral of 2x with respect to x is x² + C..."
```

**Tips:**

- Be specific with your questions for better responses
- Use follow-up questions to dive deeper into topics
- The AI remembers conversation context

### 2.  AI Study Planner

**What it does:** Generate personalized study plans based on your subject, available time, and learning goals.

**How to use:**

1. Navigate to the **"Study Planner"** section
2. Fill in the form:
   - **Subject**: What you want to study (e.g., "Mathematics", "Biology")
   - **Duration**: How many weeks you want to study
   - **Difficulty**: Easy, Intermediate, or Advanced
   - **Goals**: Specific learning objectives (optional)
3. Click **"Generate Study Plan"**
4. Review and customize your generated plan

**Sample Study Plan Output:**

```
## Week 1: Introduction to Calculus
### Daily Schedule:
- **Monday**: Basic concepts and definitions (2 hours)
- **Tuesday**: Limits and continuity (2 hours)
- **Wednesday**: Review and practice problems (1 hour)
- **Thursday**: Derivatives introduction (2 hours)
- **Friday**: Applications of derivatives (2 hours)
### Resources:
- Khan Academy videos on calculus basics
- Stewart Calculus textbook chapters 1-3
### Assessment:
- Weekly quiz on fundamental concepts
```

### 3.  AI Quiz Generator

**What it does:** Create custom quizzes and assessments for any subject or topic.

**How to use:**

1. Go to the **"Quiz Generator"** section
2. Provide quiz details:
   - **Topic**: Specific subject matter (e.g., "Photosynthesis")
   - **Subject**: Broader category (e.g., "Biology")
   - **Difficulty**: Easy, Intermediate, or Advanced
   - **Number of Questions**: How many questions to generate
3. Click **"Generate Quiz"**
4. Take the quiz or save it for later

**Quiz Format:**

- Multiple choice questions (A, B, C, D)
- Instant feedback on answers
- Explanations for correct answers
- Score tracking and progress indicators

**Example Quiz Question:**

```
What is the primary function of photosynthesis in plants?

A) Water absorption
B) Energy production through light
C) Mineral transport
D) Seed production

Correct Answer: B) Energy production through light
Explanation: Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy...
```

### 4.  Note Summarizer

**What it does:** Condense long texts, lecture notes, or research papers into concise summaries.

**How to use:**

1. Access the **"Note Summarizer"** tool
2. Paste or type the text you want summarized
3. Set the desired **summary length** (in words)
4. Click **"Summarize"**
5. Review the generated summary

**Useful for:**

- Condensing long articles or papers
- Creating study notes from lectures
- Extracting key points from research materials
- Preparing for exams or presentations

**Example:**

```
Original Text (500 words) → Summary (150 words)
The AI will identify the main ideas, key arguments, and important details while removing unnecessary information.
```

### 5. Context-Based Q&A

**What it does:** Ask questions about your uploaded documents and get answers based on the content.

**How to use:**

1. **Upload study materials** (PDF or TXT files)
2. Go to the **"Ask Questions"** section
3. Type your question about the uploaded content
4. The AI will search through your materials and provide relevant answers

**Supported file types:**

- PDF documents
- Text files (.txt)
- Research papers
- Lecture notes
- Study guides

**Example workflow:**

1. Upload your "Introduction to Psychology" textbook chapter
2. Ask: "What are the main theories of motivation discussed in this chapter?"
3. Get an answer based specifically on your uploaded content

### 6.  Flashcard Generator

**What it does:** Create interactive flashcards from your content or subject areas.

**How to use:**

1. Navigate to **"Flashcard Generator"**
2. Choose your generation method:
   - **From Content**: Paste text to generate cards from
   - **From Subject**: Specify subject and topic
3. Set preferences:
   - Number of cards to generate
   - Card type (Multiple Choice, True/False, Short Answer)
   - Difficulty level
   - Priority areas to focus on
4. Click **"Generate Flashcards"**
5. Study with your personalized flashcards

**Flashcard Features:**

- Spaced repetition algorithm
- Priority-based learning
- Multiple study modes
- Progress tracking
- Export capabilities

## Advanced Features

### Multi-Language Support

Dabba AI supports multiple languages for questions and responses:

- **English** (en) - Default language
- **Hindi** (hi) - हिंदी
- **Bengali** (bn) - বাংলা

**How to use:**

1. When asking questions, add the language code
2. The AI will respond in your preferred language
3. Example: "What is machine learning?" (English) vs "¿Qué es el aprendizaje automático?" (Spanish)

### File Upload and Processing

**Supported Formats:**

- **PDF Files**: Research papers, textbooks, lecture slides
- **TXT Files**: Notes, articles, study materials

**Upload Process:**

1. Click **"Upload Materials"** button
2. Select your file(s)
3. Wait for processing (may take a few minutes for large files)
4. Once processed, you can ask questions about the content

**File Management:**

- View uploaded files in the materials section
- Remove files when no longer needed
- Re-upload updated versions

### Model Selection

**Available AI Models:**

- **gemma3:1b** (Default) - Fast and lightweight
- **llama2:7b** - More capable but slower
- **codellama:7b** - Specialized for coding questions

**Switching Models:**

1. Go to Settings or Model Selection
2. Choose your preferred model
3. The application will use it for all AI operations

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Problem:** "Ollama service not available"

```
Solution: Ensure Ollama is installed and running:
1. Check if Ollama is installed: ollama --version
2. Start Ollama service: ollama serve
3. Verify it's running: curl http://localhost:11434/api/version
```

**Problem:** "No response from AI"

```
Solution:
1. Check your internet connection
2. Verify Ollama models are installed: ollama list
3. Try pulling the model again: ollama pull gemma3:1b
4. Restart the Ollama service
```

**Problem:** "File upload failed"

```
Solution:
1. Ensure file is PDF or TXT format
2. Check file size (limit: 50MB)
3. Try a smaller file or split large documents
4. Check available disk space
```

**Problem:** "Slow responses"

```
Solution:
1. Use the default model (gemma3:1b) for faster responses
2. Close other applications to free up resources
3. Ensure sufficient RAM (8GB minimum recommended)
4. Check if other AI models are downloading in background
```

### Getting Help

If you encounter issues:

1. **Check the status indicators** in the app for service health
2. **Review the troubleshooting section** in the main documentation
3. **Check browser console** for error messages (F12 → Console)
4. **Restart the application** and try again
5. **Contact support** if issues persist
